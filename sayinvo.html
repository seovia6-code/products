How SayInvo Works in 6 Simple Steps

From conversational prompt to payment in your bank account, experience the streamlined future of business billing.

Step 1

Prompt or Paste

Type a prompt in natural conversational English, paste an email thread, or describe the work you completed for your client.

Step 2

AI Data Extraction

SayInvo extracts client information, hours, rates, line items, and deliverables with instant accuracy.

Step 3

Template & Brand Styling

Apply your brand logo, corporate colors, and tailored payment terms with a single click.

Step 4

Multi-Currency & Taxes

Select from 50+ currencies. SayInvo auto-calculates live conversion rates, discounts, and regional VAT/tax.

Step 5

Custom SMTP Delivery

Send the invoice directly from your own Gmail, Outlook, or Zoho email address for maximum deliverability.

Step 6

1-Click Pay & Analytics

Clients pay seamlessly via credit card or PayPal. SayInvo logs payment confirmation and updates financial reports.
