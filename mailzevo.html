Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Mailzevo, AI content generation, and deliverability.

What is Mailzevo?
Mailzevo is an AI-powered email marketing platform in the Samaarav AI product ecosystem. It enables businesses to generate high-converting email copy, manage targeted subscriber lists, automate campaigns, split-test subject lines, and send via top-tier providers with real-time deliverability protection.
What email delivery providers does Mailzevo support?
Mailzevo supports SendGrid, Amazon SES, Mailgun, Postmark, Brevo (Sendinblue), and standard Gmail SMTP. You can configure multiple providers simultaneously and set up automatic fallback routing to prevent delivery disruption.
Does Mailzevo track email opens and clicks?
Yes. Mailzevo automatically tracks email opens via an invisible tracking pixel and measures click rates through secure link rewriting. Bounce handling and spam reports are captured via automated webhooks, keeping your contact database clean.
Can I generate full email campaigns with AI?
Yes. Mailzevo incorporates OpenAI GPT models fine-tuned on high-converting sales and marketing sequences. You can generate compelling subject lines, complete email bodies, and call-to-action buttons in seconds by providing a brief prompt.
How does the Spam Score Analyzer protect my sender reputation?
Before any email is broadcast, Mailzevo scans your text for spam triggers, excessive capitalization, risky links, and poor text-to-image ratios. It verifies your domain's SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records to ensure maximum inbox delivery.
