Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SeoVia data security, AI models, and subscriptions.

How does SeoVia get my website data?
SeoVia connects directly to your Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Google Search Console (GSC) through Google's official, secure OAuth 2.0 API. We only request read-only permissions to analyze clicks, impressions, queries, and traffic trends. We never modify anything in your Google accounts.
Which AI models does SeoVia use?
We support OpenAI (GPT-4o), Anthropic (Claude 3.5 Sonnet), and Google Gemini 1.5 Pro. You can choose your preferred provider in your project settings, or let our intelligent AI Router automatically choose the best model for each specific task.
Can I white-label reports for my clients?
Yes! On the Agency plan, you can fully brand executive PDF reports with your own agency logo, primary brand colors, and custom header and footer text. All SeoVia branding is completely removed on client-facing deliverables.
What happens when I run out of AI credits?
Your live Google performance dashboards, traffic charts, and existing reports remain fully accessible. AI-generated briefs and anomaly scans pause until your monthly credits reset, or you can easily purchase on-demand add-on credits with one click.
Can I cancel or change my plan anytime?
Yes, absolutely. There are no contracts or cancellation fees. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time directly from your billing settings. Your access continues until the end of your billing cycle.
